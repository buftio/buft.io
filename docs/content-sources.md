# Portfolio content

The first nine stories use Igor's supplied [résumé](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1yVdeR23Y5sJU6MKffIKWd-uo_GBjAuHN/edit), read on September 5, 2026. His [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/buftio) was behind a sign-in wall. Dates use completed ranges or a starting year rather than claiming the résumé's 'Present' is current.

Glite: voice agents, speech evaluation, Image Test and product experiments. Quantori: shared research knowledge and collaboration. Yandex: recruiter workflows and interactive content. AKTS: a seasonal launch and internal workflow automation. EPAM: navigation, comparison and annotation of 10–30 GB research images. Sperasoft: artist integrations and FIFA 2022 interfaces. Kontur: bank integrations, tax payments and business registration. Lumiprobe: molecular notation and IUPAC names. MarketData: technical audit and team workflows.

The miniature scenes are original illustrations of the work, not screenshots of employer products. Dialogue in Glite is illustrative. No confidential product screens or unverified business outcomes are included. Employer links describe the organizations; Lumiprobe links to the actual open-source repository. AKTS and MarketData use text wordmarks because exact brand assets and canonical project URLs have not been verified.

The September 7 Glite revision uses Igor's additional description of its dictionary, question feed, and picture exercise. The desert-to-dessert conversation is an original illustrative example, selected by Igor, rather than a recorded learner session. The word distinction is documented by [the Language Portal of Canada](https://our-languages.canada.ca/en/writing-tips-plus/desert-dessert-just-deserts) and Cambridge's entries for [desert](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/desert) and [dessert](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/dessert). The local quiz and small dictionary demonstrate those interactions without connecting to Glite's services.

Igor subsequently confirmed that he scaled the acquisition tests to hundreds of thousands of users per month, bringing users into the app. This is the source for the monthly test audience, distinct from app active-user figures. The résumé, read again on September 7, supports the production voice agent pipeline, speech evaluation, mixed CPU- and I/O-bound infrastructure workloads, leadership of three developers, and PostHog experiments and funnel analysis. The four-question portfolio quiz reports only its own idiom score, not an English proficiency level.

The September 7 MarketData workshop uses Igor's direct account of building the backend and frontend from scratch for a carpet factory, automating manual invoice checks, and replacing manual Excel cost calculations with software. This account replaces the earlier generic marketplace-analytics description. The clay factory, logistics, flying carpets, people, and cost sheet are original illustrations. The cost sheet uses illustrative workshop credits, not company financial data. Woven textures are generated from original patterns and the visitor's stitch drawing. Designs and production progress are saved only in that browser's local storage.
